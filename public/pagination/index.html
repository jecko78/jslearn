30-Day English Plan

Your interactive guide to starting English.

Learning Philosophy

  • Patience is Everything: A marathon, not a sprint.
  • Repetition is Key: Daily review is vital.
  • Communication First: Don't fear mistakes.
  • Keep it Fun: Use games, songs, and cartoons.

Recommended Tools

  • Apps: Duolingo, Memrise
  • YouTube: "Fun Kids English", "Peppa Pig"
  • Websites: BBC Learning English
  • Tools: Notebook, Sticky Notes

Week 3: Daily Life & Actions

Day 15: Food Vocabulary

Learn 10 basic food words like rice, water, apple, and chicken.

Day 16-17: Action Verbs

Learn verbs like eat, drink, see, want, go, sleep, read, write.

Day 18: Days of the Week

Listen to a song to learn Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday...

Day 19-21: Review & Fun

Review all words, watch a cooking video, and say the days of the week.

Week 4: Expanding Your World

Day 22-23: Places & Animals

Learn names for places (school, park) and animals (cat, dog).

Day 24: Asking "What?"

Practice the question "What is this?" and answer it.

Day 25: Asking "Where?"

Practice "Where is the...?" using a simple map you draw.

Day 26-28: Monthly Review & Fun

Review all words from the month and listen to simple English songs.
