✅ Using 'There is' and 'There are'

There is... (for one thing)

  • ✔️ There is a book on the table.
  • ❌ There isn't a pen in the box.

There are... (for many things)

  • ✔️ There are three pens in my bag.
  • ❌ There aren't any keys on the desk.
