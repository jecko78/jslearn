Prepositions of Location

Prepositions tell you where something is.

On / Over / Above

Under / Below

In

By / Near / Next to

Between

In front of

Behind

Example Sentences

Q: Where is the bird?

A: The bird is in the tree.

Q: Where are the clouds?

A: The clouds are over the volcano.

Q: Where is the school?

A: The school is between the volcano and the road.
