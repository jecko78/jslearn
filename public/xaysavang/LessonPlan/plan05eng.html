English Lesson Plan P.3

Lesson 5: Family & Descriptions

⏱️ Duration: 1 Hour

Part 1: Preparation

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦Name family members.
  • 🧍Describe people's appearance.
  • 😊Describe people's personality.

🛠️ Teaching Materials

  • 🃏Flashcards (Family, Adjectives)
  • 🖼️Picture of a family
  • 🎨Drawing paper and crayons
  • 📝Worksheet

Part 2: Lesson Flow

5Mins

Step 1: Warm-up 🎵

Sing "Finger Family". After the song, show a picture of a family and ask "Who is this?" pointing to the father, mother, etc.

15Mins

Step 2: Presentation 🧑‍🏫

Introduce family vocabulary (father, mother, brother, sister) and appearance/personality adjectives (tall, short, happy, strong) with flashcards. Model sentences: "He is my father. He is tall."

20Mins

Step 3: Practice ✍️

  • **"Describe Your Friend":** A student stands up and the class describes them: "She is short. She is happy."
  • **Family Picture Questions:** Using the family photo, ask "Is the mother tall or short?" "Is the brother strong?".
15Mins

Step 4: Production 🎨

  • **"My Family" Drawing:** Students draw a picture of their own family.
  • **Pair & Share:** In pairs, students describe one family member from their drawing: "This is my sister. She is young. She is funny."
5Mins

Step 5: Wrap-up 👋

Review family and adjective flashcards. Ask a few students, "Tell me about your father" or "Tell me about your mother". Praise the class.

Part 3: Evaluation

✔️ Check for Understanding

  • 🙋Observe participation in description games.
  • 🎤Listen for correct vocabulary and sentences.
  • 📝Check drawings for comprehension.
  • 💬Assess descriptions in pair work.
