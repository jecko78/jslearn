English Lesson Plan P.3

Lesson 20: Making Plans

⏱️ Duration: 1 Hour

Part 1: Preparation

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • 🗣️Suggest activities with "Let's...".
  • 👍Agree or disagree with plans.
  • 🗓️Role-play making a plan.

🛠️ Teaching Materials

  • 🃏Flashcards (activities: park, movie)
  • 📅A large calendar page (weekend)
  • 🧑‍🎨Whiteboard and markers
  • 📝Worksheet with a blank planner

Part 2: Lesson Flow

5Mins

Step 1: Warm-up ☀️

Review weather. Ask, "How's the weather today? It's sunny! What can we do on a sunny day?" to generate activity ideas.

15Mins

Step 2: Presentation 🧑‍🏫

Introduce phrases for making plans. Teach "What should we do this weekend?" and "Let's go to the park." Model how to agree ("Good idea!") and disagree ("I'm not sure.").

20Mins

Step 3: Practice ✍️

  • **"Let's..." Chain:** Teacher says, "Let's play football." A student agrees and adds on: "Good idea! Let's play football and eat ice cream."
  • **Calendar Planning:** Point to Saturday on the calendar. Students suggest activities using "Let's...".
15Mins

Step 4: Production 🎨

  • **My Weekend Plan:** Students fill in their worksheet planner, drawing or writing what they want to do on Saturday and Sunday.
  • **Pair Planning:** In pairs, students make a plan for the weekend together. "What should we do on Saturday?" "Let's go to the zoo." "Good idea!"
5Mins

Step 5: Wrap-up 👋

Ask the class, "What is our plan for the next English class?". Praise their great planning skills and teamwork.

Part 3: Evaluation

✔️ Check for Understanding

  • 🙋Observe confidence in making suggestions.
  • 🎤Listen for correct use of "Let's...".
  • 📝Check worksheet for logical plans.
  • 💬Assess interaction during pair planning.
