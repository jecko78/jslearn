English Lesson Plan P.3

Lesson 6: In the Classroom

⏱️ Duration: 1 Hour

Part 1: Preparation

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • ✏️Name classroom objects.
  • 👉Use "this/that" & "these/those".
  • 🧍Follow classroom commands.

🛠️ Teaching Materials

  • 🃏Flashcards (Classroom objects)
  • 📖Real classroom objects (book, pen)
  • 🧑‍🎨Whiteboard and markers
  • 📝Worksheet

Part 2: Lesson Flow

5Mins

Step 1: Warm-up 👀

Play "I Spy" using objects and colors. ("I spy with my little eye, something blue." Answer: "The door!").

15Mins

Step 2: Presentation 🧑‍🏫

Introduce vocabulary with flashcards. Demonstrate "This is a pen" (holding it) vs. "That is the board" (pointing). Do the same with plural "These are my books" vs. "Those are the chairs." Introduce commands: Stand up, Sit down, Be quiet.

20Mins

Step 3: Practice ✍️

  • **"Teacher Says":** Play "Simon Says" with classroom commands. ("Teacher says, stand up." "Touch your desk.")
  • **Pointing Game:** Teacher points to near/far objects and asks "What's this?" or "What's that?". Students answer.
15Mins

Step 4: Production 🎨

  • **Worksheet:** Students match pictures of classroom objects to their names.
  • **Pair Commands:** In pairs, one student gives a command ("Point to the window") and the other follows it.
5Mins

Step 5: Wrap-up 👋

Do a quick-fire review with flashcards. Ask the whole class to stand up, then sit down. Praise their listening skills.

Part 3: Evaluation

✔️ Check for Understanding

  • 🙋Observe participation in "Teacher Says".
  • 🎤Listen for correct use of this/that/these/those.
  • 📝Check worksheet for accuracy.
  • 💬Assess if students can follow commands in pairs.
