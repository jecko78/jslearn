English Lesson Plan P.3

Lesson 13: Abilities & Hobbies (Can/Can't)

⏱️ Duration: 1 Hour

Part 1: Preparation

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • 👍Use "can" and "can't" for ability.
  • ❓Ask "Can you...?" questions.
  • 🎨Talk about hobbies and skills.

🛠️ Teaching Materials

  • 🃏Flashcards (Hobbies: sing, dance, swim)
  • 🥎A soft ball
  • 🧑‍🎨Whiteboard and markers
  • 📝Worksheet

Part 2: Lesson Flow

5Mins

Step 1: Warm-up 🎵

Sing "If You're Happy and You Know It." After, ask students "Can you clap your hands?" to introduce the word "can."

15Mins

Step 2: Presentation 🧑‍🏫

Show hobby flashcards and model sentences: "I can sing." Mime an action you can't do (e.g., fly) and say "I can't fly." Teach the question "Can you swim?" and answers "Yes, I can" / "No, I can't."

20Mins

Step 3: Practice ✍️

  • **"Can You?" Game:** Teacher does an action (e.g., touch your nose) and asks "Can you do this?". Students who can, do the action.
  • **Ball Toss Questions:** Toss a ball to a student and ask "Can you dance?". They answer and toss the ball to another student, asking a new question.
15Mins

Step 4: Production 🎨

  • **My Abilities Worksheet:** Students draw or write three things they can do and one thing they can't do.
  • **Pair & Share:** In pairs, students share their abilities: "I can draw and I can run. I can't sing."
5Mins

Step 5: Wrap-up 👋

Do a quick poll. "Raise your hand if you can swim." "Raise your hand if you can sing." Praise the students for sharing their talents.

Part 3: Evaluation

✔️ Check for Understanding

  • 🙋Observe participation in the ball game.
  • 🎤Listen for correct use of "can" and "can't".
  • 📝Check worksheet for comprehension.
  • 💬Assess ability to share abilities in pairs.
