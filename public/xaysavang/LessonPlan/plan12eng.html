English Lesson Plan P.3

Lesson 12: Daily Activities & Simple Present

⏱️ Duration: 1 Hour

Part 1: Preparation

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • 🌞Name daily activities.
  • 🗣️Use Simple Present tense.
  • 📅Describe daily routines.

🛠️ Teaching Materials

  • 🃏Flashcards (Daily activities)
  • ⏰A large clock with movable hands
  • 🧑‍🎨Whiteboard and markers
  • 📝Worksheet with clock faces

Part 2: Lesson Flow

5Mins

Step 1: Warm-up 🕰️

Review telling time. Show different times on the big clock and ask the class "What time is it?". This connects time to daily activities.

15Mins

Step 2: Presentation 🧑‍🏫

Introduce daily activities (wake up, eat breakfast, go to school) with flashcards and actions. Teach the Simple Present: "I wake up at 7 o'clock." / "He goes to school at 8 o'clock."

20Mins

Step 3: Practice ✍️

  • **Mime Game:** A student mimes an activity (e.g., brushing teeth), and the class guesses, "You brush your teeth."
  • **Daily Routine Chant:** Teacher says an activity and a time, students chant the full sentence back. "Wake up, 7 o'clock!" -> "I wake up at 7 o'clock!"
15Mins

Step 4: Production 🎨

  • **My Day Worksheet:** Students draw pictures of their daily activities next to clock faces showing the time.
  • **Pair & Share:** In pairs, students describe their day to a partner: "I eat breakfast at 7 o'clock. Then I go to school."
5Mins

Step 5: Wrap-up 👋

Review activity flashcards quickly. Ask a few students, "What do you do in the evening?" Praise the class for describing their day.

Part 3: Evaluation

✔️ Check for Understanding

  • 🙋Observe participation in mime game and chant.
  • 🎤Listen for correct use of Simple Present.
  • 📝Check worksheet for logical routine.
  • 💬Assess ability to describe their day in pairs.
