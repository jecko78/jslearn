English Lesson Plan P.3

Lesson 8: Location & Commands

⏱️ Duration: 1 Hour

Part 1: Preparation

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • 📍Use location words (here, there).
  • 🏃Follow and give simple commands.
  • 🚫Use negative commands (Don't...).

🛠️ Teaching Materials

  • 🃏Flashcards (Commands: Go, Stop, Look)
  • 🤫An object to hide (e.g., a special eraser)
  • 🧑‍🎨Whiteboard and markers
  • 📝Worksheet

Part 2: Lesson Flow

5Mins

Step 1: Warm-up 🏃‍♀️

Play a quick game of "Teacher Says" using commands from the previous lesson (Stand up, Sit down, Touch your desk).

15Mins

Step 2: Presentation 🧑‍🏫

Introduce new commands (Go, Stop, Come, Look, Touch) by acting them out. Demonstrate location words by saying "Come here" and "Go there." Introduce negative commands: "Go to the door. Don't stop."

20Mins

Step 3: Practice ✍️

  • **"Teacher Says" with New Commands:** Play the game again using the new vocabulary like "Go," "Stop," and "Look."
  • **Obstacle Course:** Set up a simple course. "Go to the desk. Look under the chair. Stop."
15Mins

Step 4: Production 🎨

  • **Treasure Hunt:** Hide an object. Give a student commands to find it. "Go to the front. Look on the table. Don't look in the bag."
  • **Drawing Directions:** Students draw a simple map of the classroom and draw a path from their desk to the door.
5Mins

Step 5: Wrap-up 👋

Give a series of quick commands to the whole class: "Everybody, look at the board. Don't look at me. Stop! Good job!" Praise their excellent listening.

Part 3: Evaluation

✔️ Check for Understanding

  • 🙋Observe if students follow multi-step commands.
  • 🎤Listen for understanding of "here" vs "there".
  • 📝Check worksheet for drawing accuracy.
  • 💬Assess if students can respond to negative commands.
