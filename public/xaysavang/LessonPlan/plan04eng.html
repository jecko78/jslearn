English Lesson Plan P.3

Lesson 4: Negatives & Questions

⏱️ Duration: 1 Hour

Part 1: Preparation

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • 🙅Use negative forms of "To Be".
  • ❓Ask Yes/No and "Or" questions.
  • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑Introduce another person simply.

🛠️ Teaching Materials

  • 🃏Flashcards (Jobs, Countries)
  • 🖼️Pictures of different people
  • 🧑‍🎨Whiteboard and markers
  • 📝Worksheet

Part 2: Lesson Flow

5Mins

Step 1: Warm-up 🤔

Review "To Be" from Lesson 3. Ask a student: "Are you a teacher?". Elicit "No, I am not. I am a student." Write "am not" on the board.

15Mins

Step 2: Presentation 🧑‍🏫

Introduce negative forms "is not (isn't)" and "are not (aren't)". Model questions: "Is he a doctor?" and "Is she a teacher or a student?". Show how to introduce someone: "This is my friend, Kim."

20Mins

Step 3: Practice ✍️

  • **"Who Am I?" Game:** Teacher thinks of a class member. Students ask Yes/No questions: "Is it a boy?", "Is he wearing glasses?".
  • **Picture Questions:** Show a picture of a person. Students ask questions: "Is he a farmer or a doctor?".
15Mins

Step 4: Production 🎨

  • **"My Imaginary Friend" Worksheet:** Students fill in details about an imaginary friend (name, age, job).
  • **Pair Introductions:** In pairs, students introduce their imaginary friends to each other: "This is my friend, Leo. He is not a teacher, he is a student."
5Mins

Step 5: Wrap-up 👋

Teacher holds up a flashcard of a doctor and asks, "Is she a teacher?" to elicit "No, she isn't. She is a doctor." Praise the class for their great work.

Part 3: Evaluation

✔️ Check for Understanding

  • 🙋Observe participation in guessing games.
  • 🎤Listen for correct use of "isn't/aren't".
  • 📝Check worksheet for accuracy.
  • 💬Assess introductions during pair work.
