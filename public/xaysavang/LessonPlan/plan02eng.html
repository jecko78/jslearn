English Lesson Plan P.3

Lesson 2: Numbers, Age, & Phone Numbers

⏱️ Duration: 1 Hour

Part 1: Preparation

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • 🔢Count and identify numbers 0-100.
  • 🎂Ask and answer about age.
  • 📞Ask and say phone numbers.

🛠️ Teaching Materials

  • 🃏Number Flashcards (0-20, 30, 40...)
  • 칠Whiteboard & Markers
  • 🎲Bingo Cards & Number tokens
  • 📝Worksheet

Part 2: Lesson Flow

5Mins

Step 1: Warm-up 🎵

Sing a number song like "Ten Little Numbers" or "1, 2, Buckle My Shoe" to get students energized and thinking about numbers.

15Mins

Step 2: Presentation 🧑‍🏫

Introduce numbers 0-20, then tens (30, 40, 50...). Teach the questions: "How old are you?" (I am ... years old) and "What's your phone number?" (My number is...).

20Mins

Step 3: Practice ✍️

  • **Number Bingo:** Call out numbers randomly and have students mark them on their cards.
  • **Age Chain:** Students ask the person next to them, "How old are you?" and they answer, creating a chain around the room.
15Mins

Step 4: Production 🎨

  • **My Info Card:** Students create a small card with their name, age, and a made-up phone number.
  • **Pair & Share:** In pairs, students practice asking for and giving their information using the cards.
5Mins

Step 5: Wrap-up 👋

Quickly review numbers by having the class count up to 20 together. Ask a few students their age to check understanding. Praise everyone.

Part 3: Evaluation

✔️ Check for Understanding

  • 🙋Observe participation in games.
  • 🎤Listen for correct number pronunciation.
  • 📝Check worksheet for accuracy.
  • 💬Assess asking/answering in pair work.
