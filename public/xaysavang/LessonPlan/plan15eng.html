English Lesson Plan P.3

Lesson 15: Telling the Time

⏱️ Duration: 1 Hour

Part 1: Preparation

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • ❓Ask "What time is it?".
  • 🕰️Tell the time using "o'clock".
  • ✋Identify the hour hand.

🛠️ Teaching Materials

  • 🃏Flashcards (Clock faces)
  • ⏰A large demonstration clock
  • 🧑‍🎨Whiteboard and markers
  • 📝Worksheet with blank clocks

Part 2: Lesson Flow

5Mins

Step 1: Warm-up 🔢

Review numbers 1-12 by counting around the classroom. Point to numbers on a clock face as you count.

15Mins

Step 2: Presentation 🧑‍🏫

Introduce the demonstration clock. Explain the short hand (hour) and long hand (minute). Teach the question "What time is it?" and the answer structure "It's ___ o'clock." Model with several examples.

20Mins

Step 3: Practice ✍️

  • **"What Time Is It, Mr. Wolf?":** Play the classic game where students ask the time and take steps forward.
  • **Clock Drill:** Teacher sets a time on the demonstration clock, and students call out the time together.
15Mins

Step 4: Production 🎨

  • **Clock Worksheet:** Students draw the hands on blank clock faces to match times like "3 o'clock" and "9 o'clock."
  • **Pair & Share:** One student draws a time and asks their partner, "What time is it?". The partner answers.
5Mins

Step 5: Wrap-up 👋

Set the clock to various times and have the class shout the answers. Relate times to daily activities: "It's 12 o'clock! Time for lunch!"

Part 3: Evaluation

✔️ Check for Understanding

  • 🙋Observe participation in the "Mr. Wolf" game.
  • 🎤Listen for correct pronunciation of "o'clock".
  • 📝Check worksheet for correct hand placement.
  • 💬Assess asking and answering in pair work.
