English Lesson Plan P.3

Lesson 1: Greetings & Introductions

⏱️ Duration: 1 Hour

Part 1: Get Ready

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • 👋Greet and say goodbye correctly
  • ❓Ask and answer about names and well-being
  • 🔤Sing the ABC song and spell their own name

🛠️ Teaching Materials

  • 🃏Flashcards
  • 📈ABC Song Chart
  • ⚽1 Soft Ball
  • 🎵Music Player

Part 2: Lesson Flow

5Mins

Step 1: Warm-up 🎶

The teacher greets students ("Hello everyone!") and plays a "Hello Song" for students to sing and act along to create a fun atmosphere.

15Mins

Step 2: Presentation 🗣️

Teach greeting vocabulary (Hello, Good morning) and question-answer phrases (How are you? / I'm fine., What is your name? / My name is...) using flashcards and gestures.

20Mins

Step 3: Practice 🤝

  • **Greeting Pairs:** Students practice conversation with a partner.
  • **Name Ball Game:** Pass a ball in a circle while asking and answering names.
  • **ABC Song & Spelling:** Sing the song and practice spelling their own names.
15Mins

Step 4: Production 🎭

  • **Role Play:** Students come to the front to perform a short dialogue.
  • **Find a New Friend:** Walk around to meet and introduce themselves to new friends.
5Mins

Step 5: Wrap-up 👋

The teacher reviews all vocabulary and phrases, then teaches farewells (Goodbye, See you later) and says goodbye together.

Part 3: Evaluation

✔️ Check for Understanding

  • 🙋Observe participation in activities.
  • 🎤Assess pronunciation accuracy.
  • 💬Evaluate ability in short conversations.
