Conversational English - Lesson 19

Shopping for Clothes

Asking for Help

"Excuse me, I'm looking for a jacket."

"Do you have this in a size medium?"

"Can I try this on?"

Vocabulary: In a Clothes Shop

Common Items:

shirt, pants, dress, shoes, skirt, jacket

Sizes & Price:

small, medium, large

"How much is this?"

Example Dialogue

Shop Assistant: Can I help you?

You: Yes, I'm looking for a red dress.

Shop Assistant: What size are you?

You: I'm a small. How much is it?

Shop Assistant: It's $40.

Practice

  • What are you wearing today?
  • What is your favorite color for clothes?
  • Practice asking for a pair of blue pants in your size.
