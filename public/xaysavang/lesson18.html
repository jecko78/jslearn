Conversational English - Lesson 18

Ordering Food in a Restaurant

Getting a Table

"A table for two, please."

"Do you have a table for four?"

How to Order

When the waiter comes to your table, you can use these phrases.

To say what you want:

"I'd like the chicken."

"I'll have the soup, please."

To ask a question:

"What do you recommend?"

"What is in this dish?"

Example Dialogue

Waiter: Hello, are you ready to order?

You: Yes, I'll have the fish and chips.

Waiter: Anything to drink?

You: Just water for me, please.

Practice

  • What is your favorite food to order at a restaurant?
  • Practice ordering a drink: "I'd like a..."
  • Ask a partner: "What would you like to eat?"
