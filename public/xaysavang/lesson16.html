Conversational English - Lesson 16

Talking About the Weather

Asking About the Weather

"What's the weather like today?"

"How's the weather?"

Vocabulary: Weather Words

Here are some common words to describe the weather.

Sunny

It's sunny today.

Cloudy

It's cloudy.

Rainy

It's raining.

Windy

It's windy outside.

Snowy

It's snowing.

Hot

It's very hot!

Answering the Question

The structure for your answer is simple: It's + (adjective).

Q: What's the weather like?

A: It's sunny and hot.

Q: How's the weather?

A: It's cold and cloudy today.

Practice

  • What is the weather like in your city today?
  • What is your favorite type of weather?
  • Is it hot or cold today?
