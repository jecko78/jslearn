Conversational English - Lesson 17

Asking For & Giving Directions

Asking for Directions

"Excuse me, how do I get to the library?"

"Where is the nearest post office?"

Vocabulary: Direction Words

Use these phrases to give directions.

Turn left

Turn right

Go straight ahead

On the corner

Next to

Across from

Giving Directions: Examples

Q: How do I get to the bank?

A: Go straight ahead for two blocks, then turn right. The bank is on the corner.

Q: Where is the supermarket?

A: Turn left at the next street. It's next to the big bookstore.

Practice

  • How do you get from your house to the market?
  • Give directions from our classroom to the front gate.
  • Is there a restaurant near your home? Where is it?
