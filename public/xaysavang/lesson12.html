Conversational English - Lesson 12

Daily Activities & The Simple Present Tense

Vocabulary: Daily Activities

wake up eat breakfast go to work/school study cook dinner watch TV go to sleep

Grammar: Simple Present Tense

Use this for actions that are habits, routines, or facts. (e.g., every day, often, sometimes)

I / You / We / They

Use the base form of the verb.

"I go to school every day."

He / She / It

Add -s or -es to the verb.

"She goes to work every day."

Negatives & Questions

Use "do" and "does" to make negative sentences and questions.

Negative: They do not (don't) watch TV.

Negative: He does not (doesn't) cook dinner.

Question: Do you study English?

Question: Does she wake up early?

Practice

  • What do you do every morning?
  • Do you watch TV at night?
  • Does your friend study English?
