Conversational English - Lesson 6

In the Classroom

Classroom Vocabulary

Desk Paper Blackboard Chalk Notebook Eraser Pen Pencil Backpack

This, These, That & Those

Near (Here)

This (Singular)

What is this? This is a pen.

These (Plural)

What are these? These are my books.

Far (There)

That (Singular)

What is that? That is the blackboard.

Those (Plural)

What are those? Those are desks.

Classroom Commands

  • ‣ Sit down.
  • ‣ Stand up.
  • ‣ Quiet please.
  • ‣ Repeat.
  • ‣ Listen.
  • ‣ Raise your hand.

Dialogue Example

Joe: Salim, what are those?

Salim: These are my books for school.

Joe: Are you a student? I thought you were a businessman.

Salim: I am a businessman, but I am also a student.
