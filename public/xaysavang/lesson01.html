Greetings

  • Hello! / Hi!
  • Good morning!
  • Good afternoon!
  • Good evening!

Leaving Phrases

  • Goodbye / Bye-bye
  • See you later / tomorrow.
  • Have a good day / night!
  • Good night!

Key Questions

How are you?

→ I'm fine.

What is your name?

→ My name is...

Dialogue Example

John: Hello.

Sarah: Hi.

John: How are you?

Sarah: I'm fine. And you?

John: I'm fine. My name is John. What's your name?

Sarah: My name's Sarah. It's nice to meet you.

John: It's nice to meet you too.
