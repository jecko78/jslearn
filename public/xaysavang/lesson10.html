The Verb "To Have"

Use "to have" to talk about possessions.

HAVE

I / You / We / They

"They have a beautiful dining room."

HAS

He / She / It

"She has a nice kitchen."

Example: My House

I live in a small house. It has one bedroom with a closet. The kitchen has a stove and a refrigerator. There is a couch and a table in the living room. I don't have a dining room.

Practice Questions

  • What do you have in your kitchen?
  • How many bedrooms do you have in your house?
  • Do you have any decorations in your house?
