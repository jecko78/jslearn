Conversational English - Lesson 14

Talking About Likes & Dislikes

Vocabulary: Food & Activities

pizza ice cream reading books watching movies playing sports listening to music

Grammar: Using "Like"

Use these words to talk about things you enjoy or do not enjoy.

Positive (+)

"I like pizza."

"She likes reading books."

Negative (-)

"We don't like waking up early."

"He doesn't like ice cream."

Tip: If you use a verb after "like", add -ing. For example: "I like reading."

Asking Questions with "Do" and "Does"

Question: Do you like watching movies?

Answer: Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

Question: Does she like sports?

Answer: Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

Practice

  • What food do you like?
  • Do you like listening to music?
  • What does your friend like to do?
