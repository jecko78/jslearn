Conversational English - Lesson 20

Making Plans for the Future

Asking About Plans

"What are you doing this weekend?"

"Do you have any plans for tomorrow?"

Talking About Your Plans: "Going to"

Use "going to" to talk about future plans. The structure is: Subject + am/is/are + going to + verb

"I am going to visit my family."

"She is going to watch a movie."

"We are going to play football."

"They are going to study."

Example Dialogue

Maria: Hi Sam! What are you doing this weekend?

Sam: Hi Maria! I'm going to go to the beach with my friends.

Maria: That sounds fun!

Sam: What about you? Do you have any plans?

Maria: I'm going to visit my grandmother.

Practice

  • What are you going to do after class today?
  • What are you going to eat for dinner tonight?
  • Ask a partner about their plans for next week.
