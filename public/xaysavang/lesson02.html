Asking About Age

How old are you?

→ I am 27 years old.

How old is he/she?

→ He's 84 years old.

Asking for Phone Numbers

What's your phone number?

→ My number is 345 5062.

What's his/her number?

→ Her number is 756 9038.

Dialogue Example

Mike: How old are you Fatouma?

Fatouma: I am 20 years old, and you?

Mike: I am 24.

Fatouma: Nice to meet you.

Mike: Nice to meet you too. What's your phone number?

Fatouma: My phone number is 332 1195.
