Conversational English - Lesson 11

Clothing, Colors, & Actions Happening Now

Vocabulary: Colors

Red Blue Green Yellow White Black

Vocabulary: Clothing

shirt t-shirt pants dress shoes hat

Grammar: Present Continuous

Use this for actions that are happening right now.

Structure:

Subject + [am / is / are] + Verb-ing

Example Sentences

I am wearing a blue shirt.

He is wearing black shoes.

They are wearing hats.

Practice

  • What are you wearing?
  • What color is your shirt?
  • Is she wearing a dress?
