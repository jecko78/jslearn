Conversational English - Lesson 13

Talking About Abilities with "Can" & "Can't"

Vocabulary: Hobbies & Skills

swim cook speak English play football sing dance draw

Grammar: Using "Can" for Ability

Use "can" to say you have the ability to do something. Use "can't" (can not) to say you don't have the ability.

The structure is the same for all subjects (I, you, he, she, it, we, they).

Can (Ability)

"I can speak English."

"They can play football."

Can't (No Ability)

"He can't (cannot) cook."

"She can't swim."

Asking Questions with "Can"

To ask a question, put "Can" at the beginning of the sentence.

Question: Can you swim?

Answer: Yes, I can. / No, I can't.

Question: Can he dance?

Answer: Yes, he can. / No, he can't.

Practice

  • What can you do?
  • Can you cook?
  • Can your friend speak English?
