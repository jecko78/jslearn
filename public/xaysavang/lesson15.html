Conversational English - Lesson 15

How to Tell the Time

Asking the Time

"What time is it?"

"What's the time?"

Vocabulary & Examples

Here are the most common ways to talk about time.

O'clock & Half Past

It's seven o'clock. (7:00)

It's half past two. (2:30)

Quarter Past & Quarter To

It's a quarter past nine. (9:15)

It's a quarter to one. (12:45)

Saying Other Times (with minutes)

You can also just say the hour and the minutes.

8:05 ➔ It's eight oh five.

10:20 ➔ It's ten twenty.

4:50 ➔ It's four fifty.

Practice

  • What time is it right now?
  • What time do you eat breakfast?
  • What time do you go to sleep?
