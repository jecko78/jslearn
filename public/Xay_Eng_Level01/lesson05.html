Family Vocabulary & Descriptions

Family Vocabulary

Male Female Together
Father (dad) Mother (mom) Parents
Husband Wife
Brother Sister Siblings
Son Daughter Children (kids)
Grandfather Grandmother Grandparents

Appearance

What does he/she look like?

  • tall vs. short
  • handsome/beautiful vs. ugly
  • thin vs. fat
  • old vs. young
  • weak vs. strong

Personality

What is he/she like?

  • friendly vs. grumpy
  • funny vs. serious
  • nice vs. mean
  • shy vs. outgoing
  • smart vs. dumb

Dialogue Example

Ahmed: Do you know my brother?

Rose: What does he look like?

Ahmed: He is tall and thin.

Rose: Is he very smart and serious?

Ahmed: Yes.

Rose: I know him. He is very handsome.
