Conversational English - Lesson 3

"To Be", Professions, Nationality & Marital Status

The Verb "To Be"

Singular

  • I am (I'm)
  • You are (You're)
  • He/She/It is (He's/She's/It's)

Plural

  • We are (We're)
  • You are (You're)
  • They are (They're)

Professions

What do you do?

student teacher doctor farmer shopkeeper

→ I am a doctor.

→ He is a taxi driver.

→ We are farmers.

Nationality

Where are you from?

I am from the Comoros.

→ I am Comorian.

She is from France.

→ She is French.

Marital Status

Are you married?

Single Engaged Married Divorced

Q: Are you married?

A: Yes, I'm married. / No, not yet. I'm single.

Q: Is he married?

A: Yes, he is married. / No, but he is engaged.
